# PRSE — decisive source-grounded 3D support feasibility and failure decomposition
25 October 2026 — independent, real-source diagnostic, Round 2 of 5

## Original owner questions and usable decision

The August 13 and October 9 original owner PRSE recordings prioritize a persistent world described in ordinary literary text, transformed into ONE authoritative three-dimensional physical GameCore/Rapier scene near the player, then back into truthful, coherent and identity-preserving current descriptions after real collisions, contacts, forces, damage, hidden offscreen consequences and rematerialization. Renderer-independent interfaces can show text, 2D or 3D, but the core must preserve actual three-dimensional physics. Player-trained macro/habit learning and scripted jump exercises are SECONDARY.

**Direct conclusion:** PRSE has useful verified physical, source-history and observer-state infrastructure, but does NOT reliably materialize the original author's intended world. The previous real native-source data had 197 accepted scenes and only 565 of 1,237 independently annotated object-count requirements satisfied (45.7%). An earlier source-derived causal study demonstrated 32/32 real native physics/offscreen/source/observer round trips, and the recent source-event correction eliminated 82 false tipping events without changing physical motions. These do NOT prove general source meaning. The decisive open question for this round is what blocks actual author-described ON relationships: missing object kinds, insufficient physically suitable sizes, or incorrect placements even after suitable objects exist.

This round independently reuses the SAME previously frozen real GameCore/Rapier scenes and separate SceneEval author annotations. It does not rewrite, overfit, modify or create new physics to make annotation answers true.

## Frozen physical methods and equal-source but unequal-object-size comparison

Three fully independently frozen source-to-REAL-native-GameCore/Rapier world methods on the exact same 200 external source IDs were compared, with 197 accepted worlds in EACH method and three original geometry-rejected worlds intentionally kept unverified.

| Frozen source scene method | Native 3D physical bodies | Actual inter-object Rapier contact pairs |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Original source interpreter and layout | 938 | 11 |
| Original plus pretrained noun candidates represented by small generic Rapier boxes | 2,048 | 11 |
| Original plus existing physical-size-catalog-supported nouns only | 955 | 11 |

All three methods represent genuine native rigid bodies where materialized, but the extra 1,110 generic bodies are UNCALIBRATED little box approximations rather than physically correct furniture, utensils, lamps or shelves. The strict catalog adds only 17 existing-size class bodies. These are MATCHED SOURCE SAMPLES, **NOT equal object-count budgets or matched model-call/cost comparisons**. No new LLM or GPU inference was used in this study.

The official 200-description sample has **363** author-annotated ON requirements, of which **354** belong to the 197 physically accepted scenes. The other nine belong to the three originally geometry-rejected scenes and were NOT counted as successes or failures.

Each ON annotation identifies a supporting object kind, a supported object kind and an exact/at-least/greater-than quantity. For every existing REAL Rapier scene, a source-independent posthoc scorer checks:
- whether BOTH required physical category kinds exist at all;
- whether enough supported-object INSTANCES exist for the author-required quantity;
- whether existing collider bounds contain suitable support surfaces for the child's footprint (optimistic and simplified);
- whether a suitable top surface has any nominally unoccupied position without overlapping other existing bodies under transparent axis-aligned bounding box geometry;
- and, separately, whether an actual GameCore/Rapier collider-contact pair exists at the proper native three-dimensional height, footprint and upright orientation.

The final criterion is the ONLY physically achieved contact. A prospective empty AABB top position is not an actual simulated placement, asset, source reference resolution or physical success, because no bodies were moved or newly stepped for the diagnostic. Original source reference identity remains ambiguous across repeated same-type mentions and the category-pair score is optimistic.

The original physics has **307/354 (86.7%)** author ON failures because one or both required object TYPES do not exist. It has both required types in only 47/354 cases. Thirty-seven have enough existing size-compatible children to support a hypothetical body contact; thirty-five have nominally unobstructed AABB top positions; **only three actually make the required native physical contact**. This disproves claims that merely optimizing positions can generally fix the original scene converter: most missing objects are not available to place.

The broad pretrained noun expansion changes the failure composition, but not actual author source contact. Both required types exist in **226/354** instead of 47. Yet **72** now lack a sufficiently sized support footprint, **eight** have only obstructed nominal top positions, and **131** are nominally positionable in our highly simplified bounds but do not actually contact the specified supporting object in the frozen native scene. Only **three of 354** match author support with actual Rapier contact. In other words, the broad vocabulary stage helps category recall but exposes two new limits: tiny uncalibrated boxes make for wrong support geometry, and an arbitrary floor-grid placement is NOT source-conditioned physical relation realization.

The strict existing category-dimension catalog adds 17 bodies but changes NONE of these author ON results. This is important because it shows that the existing category asset/physical-surface catalog barely covers the categories that the source describes.

The top missing source support kinds in the original native scenes included vanity (20 clauses), sideboard (16), side table (10) and kitchen island (8). Frequently missing supported child categories included candle (8), mouse (7), table lamp (6), keyboard (5), fruit bowl (5) and desk lamp (4). These come only from previously independently annotated scene requirements; they were NOT inserted into a tuned parser or model during this diagnostic.

## Statistical limits and source fidelity

The scorer resampled individual SOURCE SCENES, not correlated author ON requirements, 4,000 times with fixed seed. For the original scenes, the observed fraction of author ON clauses with BOTH kinds present was 47/354 (13.3%), with an exploratory scene-bootstrap 95% interval about 9.7–17.3%. In the generic expansion it was 226/354 (63.8%), conditional interval about 56.9–70.8%. Actual native support stayed 3/354 (0.85%) in all methods, with conditional resampling ranges roughly 0–1.9%. The hypothetical collision-free AABB-top proportion was 35/354 original and 134/354 generic, *not* actual physics success.

The corpus was already examined earlier in this project, so these conditional bootstrap ranges are NOT pristine future-domain generalization estimates or evidence of a calibrated model confidence. Source ON type-pair annotations do not identify each persistent object instance uniquely, and simplified box footprints are NOT adequate evidence of actual support for rotated compound meshes or containers.

The new scorer checks exact original gold source IDs, held-out/rejected geometry coverage, per-case quantities, actual contact manifolds, known independent baseline author source ON matches, the source- and physical-world raw SHA-256 hashes, and owner-private permissions. It preserves existing prior source-count and spatial negative findings. A failed source hypothesis must not be converted into an authority or overwritten merely because a posthoc analysis finds a geometrically plausible spot.

## Owner-useful operational decision

This study resolves a practical sequencing question:

1. **Fix original object identification and instance references first.** In the original converter, 307 of 354 source ON requirements are missing a necessary object kind. A position optimizer cannot move an object that was never materialized.
2. **Require physically appropriate 3D category geometry and real support/containment affordances.** The broad noun expansion gains type recall yet leaves 72 ON requirements without sufficient existing support size. Small placeholder cubes must not be advertised as realistic tables, shelving, keyboards or candles.
3. **Add source-conditioned constraints to actual native GameCore layout.** In the generic corpus, 131 ON requirements have nominally available box-top positions but no matching true Rapier contact. A constraint-conditioned solver could in principle change this, but that upper bound requires a subsequent REAL physical placement-and-step experiment with source-only or independently learned source relations — not posthoc author-gold movement.
4. **Preserve historical source and ambiguous referents; verify contacts/events and observer knowledge.** The previously tested hypothesis ledger, native whole-world/observer restoration, bounded offscreen story processes and explicit separation of witnessed history from current factual prose are necessary and reusable. They do not fix wrong input object types, shapes, quantities or author intended relations.
5. **Then evaluate truly current literary rewriting and comprehension on untouched new real source descriptions.** Any generative LLM phrasing should be a rejectable proposal checked against original source and physical facts. Do not count identical source/physics snapshots or a fluent-looking paragraph as original source entailment.

The feasible near-term deliverable is a factually qualified 3D/native-to-semantic research core, NOT a push-button system for turning arbitrary novels into fully realistic playable worlds. No live production PRSE integration or GPU/LLM/human quality experiment was performed in this round, and no additional calendar events were created.
