## NEW physical-split experiment, not a fresh independent corpus

The official SceneEval-500 v250610 annotation ZIP (SHA-256 `2948037f497c81cb2ce8241010c8d93250248217ca9961aa33e59092196b3676`) supplies **original independently prepared author object-count and ON relation labels**. Existing research already examined this corpus. The earlier exact physical comparison used the first 100 fully human-written scenes plus every fourth assisted scene, producing 200 inputs and 197 physically admitted scenes.

This round selected **the remaining 300 partly machine-assisted source descriptions**, using deterministic indices 100–499 not divisible by four relative to 100; they are disjoint from the 200 previous *physical* inputs. They are **not** truly held-out human literary descriptions, nor a new external test distribution. This distinction is critical.

The source-only stage reused the already downloaded CPU spaCy 3.8 parser, existing frozen source lexical quantities, and earlier source-only open noun candidates. It inspected source text and provenance offsets only: **no official author ON/ObjCount annotations were read during proposal creation or GameCore physics**. Its outputs were frozen owner-privately before a separate author-gold evaluator ran. The source parser yielded **182 ON proposals across 95 of 300 descriptions**; 169 candidates belonged to 293 admitted native scenes.

Every physically admitted source world was compiled with the same original source-linked base scene and deterministic generic additional bodies. A truly native GameCore/Rapier baseline was stepped for 38 ticks. The alternative started from the identical scene and identical rigid-body names/IDs, types, sizes, collision shapes and initial placements, apart from source-only ON proposals admitted by pre-existing geometry tests. It also stepped 38 real native ticks. Every actual contact is a Rapier collider contact, not a nominal `supportId` string, AABB possibility, or made-up position. Alternative save/reload was checked against actual contacts and source history. No gold label drove a placement.

An additional 38 author ON clauses belonged to the seven geometry-rejected original scenes, and were **unverified**, not counted as failures or successes. Among the 698 evaluable requirements, only 417 had enough actual physical instances of the referenced object categories. This leaves **281 without sufficient objects/kinds**, and even among the 417 type-and-quantity-eligible clauses, **402 lacked verified support after source placement**. This is an end-to-end source-fidelity deficit; it is not proof that the parser alone caused all failures.

Of the 33 new physically admitted placements, 11 have a matching author ON type-pair annotation. Ten satisfy previously unsupported complete author ON clauses, because author requirements may include multiplicity or repeated category instances. The **other 22 new contacts have no corresponding author ON type-pair annotation**. These are source-authenticity UNKNOWN, *not* measured false positives: the external ON annotations are not a complete exhaustive list of every physically legal contact, and repeated-instance referents lack unique gold identities. The scorer did not fabricate human negatives. No expert checked the realism of these placements.

Resampling *source scenes* rather than correlated ON clauses four thousand times produced an exploratory paired supported-clause difference of **+1.43 percentage points** with a conditional 95% interval **+0.60 to +2.31 percentage points**. This interval is **not** a future-domain generalization guarantee because the entire external dataset has previously been explored and this subset is entirely partly assisted.

## Interpretation, negative controls, operational decision

The additional sample independently reproduces the **direction** of the previous bounded physical-contact gain while retaining an unchanged number of source-linked bodies and *no lost preexisting native contacts*. The new result strengthens the narrowly demonstrated method: source-only grammatical proposals → geometry feasibility → actual native Rapier contact → save/reentry can improve source-annotated support, yet most author ON clauses remain false in the physical world.

The source-only physical compiler does not yet reliably identify quantities, individual instances or coreference, and cannot ensure accurate physical dimensions. True physics contacts are **necessary, not sufficient**, for the original author scene. The all-in-text → realistic 3D → coherent literary rewriting objective remains scientifically unproved. Earlier separately frozen positive results for 13 scenes cover real impulses, qualified current physical facts, bounded offscreen processing, observer histories and identity-preserving rematerialization; **those observer/prose tests were not rerun across the new 300 scenes**. They must not be claimed here as new literary quality evidence.

Do **not** enable unrestricted autonomous literary source materialization or label parser ON proposals as author truth. Keep original text and unresolved interpretation/source references, maintain author/physical/current-event/observer distinctions, and require real 3D mesh/collider and metric-scale catalog provenance with independently tested physics support. The next result that could change the decision is a genuinely *unseen independently annotated human-written* source set with instance IDs, category, quantity, metric geometry/pose, and explicitly exhaustive support negatives, plus a separately permitted random-feasible-support baseline and human-reviewed observer prose after physical and offscreen changes. The prior denied native random-parent placebo was **not rerun** in this round.
