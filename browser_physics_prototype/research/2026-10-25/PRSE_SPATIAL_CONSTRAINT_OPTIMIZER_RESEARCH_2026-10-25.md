The main unresolved scientific question after the previous work was: why do valid PRSE physical scene plans match so few real text requirements, and can a better geometry solver measurably help when the text interpreter itself is held fixed? That is the single controlled comparison in this continuation.

## Source-reviewed scientific context

**Holodeck** — Yang et al., CVPR 2024, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Yang_Holodeck_Language_Guided_Generation_of_3D_Embodied_AI_Environments_CVPR_2024_paper.html — uses GPT-4 to propose a structured scene and relationships, then optimizes spatial placements. Its human evaluation is about that published system, not a result for PRSE.

**SceneEval** — Tam et al., WACV 2026, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/html/Tam_SceneEval_Evaluating_Semantic_Coherence_in_Text-Conditioned_3D_Indoor_Scene_Synthesis_WACV_2026_paper.html — explicitly separates text object counts/attributes/relationships from implicit physics such as collision and support. We reused the official v250610 author's ZIP already downloaded in the prior continuation and retained its published SHA.

**PAT3D** — Lin et al., ICLR 2026, https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/f35d2be7bc4da374557da1f5ef06eeeb-Abstract-Conference.html — combines text-derived scene trees with rigid-body simulation and physics-in-the-loop optimization. Its published model and visual results were not reproduced here. It gives architectural support for a proposal → constraints → physics → score/refine pipeline.

Those works motivate independently evaluating **extracted semantic relations** and **physically satisfied semantic relations**. A valid rigid-body scene by itself can be semantically nonsensical.

## Method: one frozen text interpretation, two independent geometry strategies

The input is the existing text-only PRSE deterministic parser output, **unchanged** since the prior committed benchmark. It includes exact extracted object types/counts, uncertain source relations and source-span provenance. The reference geometry generator is the existing one-pass greedy prse_constraint_layout.mjs. The alternative is the new **prse_relation_local_optimizer.mjs**, a bounded deterministic coordinate/constraint search: maximum two improvement sweeps and 150 candidate placements per scene, fixed before its first full corpus evaluation. It samples physically plausible positions adjacent, left/right, front/behind, across, over/on support, wall, center and corner without external annotations. Each candidate is rejected for interpenetration, invalid ground support or any decrease in the number of already physically feasible parsed constraints. Unsupported relations remain explicitly unresolved, not invented. No external gold annotations are available to the optimizer or the GameCore runtime.

Both strategies use the same existing PRSE GameCore/Rapier 3D world, actual rigid bodies, eighteen native steps, stable identifiers and wrapper save/reload. The old separately saved greedy physics results are the reference; a new optimized run was executed on **the identical 100 human-authored SceneEval IDs 0–99**, and on **the identical 100 assisted IDs 100,104,…,496**. The baseline and optimized source spans and object names were not altered. The raw comparison was saved under private Nitro data, with full raw SHA-256 written into safe aggregate JSON.

The physics study tests concrete extracted relationships, not image realism, texture, navigability, social world knowledge or fully fluent language. After both scene outputs were frozen, a separate scorer opened the official gold annotations and evaluated which **object-pair relation types** and **room relations** were actually satisfied by their final physical states. Even that score is **optimistic**: it ignores directed argument order and may coalesce multiple distinct object instances. It is not an exact semantic equivalence score and cannot be extrapolated to arbitrary PRSE stories. This is an exploratory corpus whose early examples and some aggregate scores were seen during earlier parser work; not a pristine hidden challenge set.

## Actual new quantitative results

| Benchmark | Physical scenes valid and restored | Extracted source constraints satisfied after Greedy → optimizer | Gold object-relations matched and physically satisfied | Gold room relations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 human-authored descriptions | 97/100 unchanged | 112 → **130** (of 188 proposed constraints; unsupported counted separately) | **22 → 30 of 478** | **38 → 39 of 290** |
| 100 sampled machine-assisted descriptions | 99/100 unchanged | 108 → **126** (of 230 proposed constraints; unsupported counted separately) | **17 → 22 of 743** | **17 → 21 of 223** |

Note the greedy/optimizer "extracted source constraints satisfied" count is over the physically instantiated/accepted scenes and may differ from the initial raw proposal score. In each group, the optimizer modified sixteen scenes and accepted eighteen individual constraint-increasing repairs. No scene lost a gold typed-pair match in this optimistic scorer, but this does not preclude unmeasured losses of visual, instance-specific, directional or navigational meaning. Two original human scenes remained geometrically rejected, one had no recognized objects; in the assisted sample one was rejected. Every accepted optimized scene preserved stable entity IDs and exact original source prose through wrapper save/reload.

The relative gains in matched annotated claims are real but **absolutely tiny**. For human-authored text, physically satisfied object-relationship recall rose from **4.60% to 6.28%**. In the assisted sample, from **2.29% to 2.96%**. Gold object-pair F-one rose from 0.0824 to 0.1089 for human text and from 0.0415 to 0.0529 for assisted text.

The earlier fixed extraction had only **40 of 478** human annotated object-pair relationships matched and only **24 of 743** assisted relationships matched before any physics. Our solver physically satisfied thirty of those forty and twenty-two of those twenty-four. Therefore **language/relation extraction, not spatial optimization, is now the dominant measured bottleneck**. A vastly better layout algorithm cannot satisfy relationships that were never extracted, or were incorrectly interpreted.

Cost: 505 bounded candidate checks for the hundred human scenes and 513 for assisted; elapsed total approx **13.4 and 14.8 seconds**, with real Rapier stepping included. Those wall times were on a busy shared Nitro CPU and are not a stable speed benchmark. The earlier greedy batches ran in less wall time but involved a different amount of extra search work. No model tokens or GPU hours were used.

## Separate correctness and negative tests

The external human-authored SceneEval room description that explicitly says **no coffee table** was compiled into a 3D scene with no coffee-table body, and stayed that way after GameCore stepping and save/reload. Two additional **synthetic** contradictory statements were tested as guards: one object cannot simultaneously satisfy both left and right of the same other object, and an explicit contradictory presence/absence of an item causes an abstention rather than inserting that object anyway. These synthetic guards do **not** count as additional independent language benchmark success.

The prior selective physical causal example remains relevant: from a real external SceneEval description, a lamp was placed on a nightstand, physically moved, and its lost/recovered support facts were propagated to source-linked semantic updates and persisted. An adverse check found that lifting vertically could leave a stale "on" relation and that an airborne body could previously be frozen by dematerializing for five seconds. Those original failures are preserved in their initial negative JSON files. The subsequent bounded research adapter checks vertical separation and actual contact before setting support, and refuses offscreen unloading when a moving object has no validated continuation. These are conservative safeguards, **not** a general solution for unseen moving-body trajectories. The currently running production game has not received the sidecar changes.

## What the comparison does and does not imply

The measured result refutes the idea that increasing the number of successfully instantiated scenes is a sufficient PRSE milestone. The earlier generic compiler could create ninety-seven or ninety-nine credible looking physical states while satisfying almost none of the independent semantic relation requirements. The new deterministic optimizer improved on-screen geometric consistency without materially solving the underlying language problem.

The next defensible experiment is a **matched, source-blind model-based semantic interpreter** that produces typed entities and signed relations from the same frozen external prompts with no gold labels available, followed by exactly the same bounded geometry validation, GameCore physics stepping, save/re-entry and factual narration checks. Evaluate object counts, negated/conditional/ambiguous facts, source span fidelity, type/direction/coreference, wall/inside/on containment and unknown-room properties independently. A model may be much better at pronouns and spatial intent, or hallucinate relations: both outcomes must be measured. Model use should wait for verifiably spare inference slots, respecting ongoing game/render/voice/agent work. A stronger relational/continuous spatial solver becomes the next priority **only after** a genuinely better parser supplies trustworthy constraints.

For the core PRSE semantic↔physical↔semantic loop, important separate gaps still remain: nontrivial genuine fracture, fire and material replacement that preserve contact and identity; observable high-speed collision/event projection; large area occlusion/perception; offscreen active actors and ongoing resource needs synchronized with one global world time; source-linked fluent literary rewriting without unsupported outcomes; and production integration with the currently concurrently dirty canonical Games main. The earlier research sidecar provides small bounded evidence of causal clock synchronization and physically grounded event/text updates, not completed PRSE gameplay.
