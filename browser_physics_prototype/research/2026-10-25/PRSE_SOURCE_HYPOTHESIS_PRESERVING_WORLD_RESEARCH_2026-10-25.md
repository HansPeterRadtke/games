# PRSE: preserving uncertain literary-source meanings without destroying real 3D evidence

25 October 2026 — continued owner PRSE research in the existing isolated Games worktree

## Direct answer to the original recording-derived questions

The central owner request, preserved in the original August 13 recordings and October 9 recordings and in Infra games/world_and_generation.md and semantic_physics_text_loop.md, is for an evolving **ordinary literary/descriptive persistent world**. Nearby scenes must materialize as ONE authoritative real 3D physical world with scaled entities, contact, forces, damage, event sequences and perceptions, then yield truthful current literary changes and continue causally while offscreen, with stable IDs on later entry. The recorded working title PRSE has no verified complete expansion; the owner said it had been forgotten. Optional player habit learning and scripted fence-jump demonstrations are not substitutes for this core scientific objective.

**What is already demonstrated:** actual GameCore/Rapier native 3D collision, physical events, stable source IDs, contact-rich native-world/semantic snapshots, bounded contact-settled offscreen time and observer-specific factual narration. Previous complete research compared sixty-three independent source-derived dynamic scenes and correctly isolated a severe reconstruction failure: a JSON pose/velocity save could reproduce identical initial coordinates but produce roughly sixty centimetres of later cup trajectory error. The research native Rapier world plus semantic transaction avoided that error in the tested full-GameCore source scenes. Subsequent native observer/source/document testing retained current entity changes, source-linked ash after an explicitly authored fire process and unobserved-event privacy. These remain valid *bounded research evidence*, not live general PRSE deployment.

**What demonstrably fails:** arbitrary story-to-scene meaning. The original source compiler correctly instantiated only roughly half the independent SceneEval object-count requirements; a broad pretrained grammar stage recovered more potential nouns but with generic uncalibrated collision boxes, no validated realistic 3D assets and little support/containment relationship fidelity. On the same 197 accepted source scenes, the original physical layouts realized only **three** of **354** author-annotated ON constraints under a generous native-contact/source-category-pair scorer. A newer source-only grammatical ON filter accepted fewer uncertain proposed relationships but, when applied destructively before physical layout, **removed one of those three actually supported author relationships**. Object-count or schema-validator success did not establish original literary intent.

The present continuation tested a safer **non-destructive and provenance-preserving interpretation ledger**: original source hypotheses survive even when the parser is uncertain; actual physical truth comes only from the native GameCore solver; only source-grammatical candidates that are ALSO physically verified may become provisional source-linked factual narration. Unresolved source interpretations are not declared false or deleted merely because this first parser cannot decide them.

## Independent published conceptual context

*SceneEval*, Tam and collaborators, WACV 2026, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/html/Tam_SceneEval_Evaluating_Semantic_Coherence_in_Text-Conditioned_3D_Indoor_Scene_Synthesis_WACV_2026_paper.html, independently evaluates source counts, attributes and spatial relationships separately from 3D physical plausibility. It does not support equating a physically valid scene with the original language meaning.

*Discourse Representation Theory*, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/discourse-representation-theory/, describes explicit discourse referents, accessibility, anaphora and the distinction between scoped descriptions and their interpretations. PRSE has NOT implemented full DRT; its simple source-indexed hypothesis ledger is an auditable engineering precursor, not a comparable semantic parser.

*A Possible Worlds Model of Belief for State-Space Narrative Planning*, Shirvani, Ware and Farrell, AIIDE 2017, https://doi.org/10.1609/aiide.v13i1.12928, models character belief as alternative epistemic possibilities in narrative planning. PRSE did NOT reproduce its full possible-world search or human-audience experiment. The paper motivates retaining uncertainty without silently overwriting observer belief with omniscient actual-world status.

## Experiment: physically identical scene plus a separate tentative source-hypothesis layer

A new **PRSEMeaningHypotheses** research sidecar wraps the pre-existing PRSESourceRevision, current verified semantic document, original source relationship proposals and the ACTUAL unchanged native GameCore/Rapier world. Unlike destructive pre-layout source ON pruning:

- Every original source relation, exact source snippet, stable source ordinal, child and proposed support relation is kept in the literary hypothesis record even if grammar cannot establish the subject/object roles.
- Source-grammatical certainty, *current physically measured* true/false/unknown and *the independent author's original intended meaning* are explicitly separate. A verified current physical support with uncertain source role remains a physical fact without being printed as a literary-source-entailed relationship.
- The narrator's source-linked ON facts are restricted to grammar-approved *and* native physically verified claims. Even an approved grammatical role is only provisional syntax evidence until independently source-entailment checked; it cannot authorize a false physics outcome.
- Original historical source and the full undecided-source ledger are saved in ONE versioned trusted physics/semantic/observer transaction. An invalid source/role or mismatch between the restored physics and claimed current relation is rejected. The existing whole-world native Rapier solver snapshot remains build-bound and owner-trusted, not a production-safe region streaming or adversarial file format.
- Offscreen semantic state preserves the original source hypotheses but explicitly marks physical contact UNKNOWN while their actual colliders are unloaded. Return regenerates the physical relation from actual Rapier contact, rather than retroactively deciding that the first sentence meant something else.

This deliberately preserves alternative SOURCE INTERPRETATIONS, not parallel fabricated entire physical worlds. It does not solve coreference, linguistic entailment, physically realistic asset retrieval, full literary prose generation, or a complete epistemic planning algorithm.

## Two hundred source-scene real 3D evaluation

Reused exactly the frozen external SceneEval original first hundred human descriptions and every-fourth one of the remaining assisted descriptions, with original native physical worlds and pretrained source syntax decisions already fixed BEFORE this study. No author gold labels entered the materialization, sidecar or experimental physics. The original three geometry-rejected source scenes remain unverified.

In all **197** previously physically accepted GameCore scenes, the new non-destructive sidecar kept **all 407** original parsed source relations, including **fifty** original source ON hypotheses. **Seven** had grammar-approved source ON roles; **forty-three** remained epistemically unresolved, and all forty-three were retained through the full native solver/source/observer/history save. Original physical measurements showed **twenty-seven** parsed ON hypotheses were true in the actual generated world. Six were grammar-approved as potential source-linked current facts; **twenty-one** were physically true but retained as linguistically unresolved, rather than being advertised as author-entailment or deleted.

All 197 saved and restored original scene text, physical IDs, native physics and source-role/hypothesis records. The selected actual GameCore action was applied in sixty-four scenes with a moving object; **eight** produced a change in the interpreted ON hypothesis truth signatures. Other physical entity changes may occur without changing the ON hypotheses; the metric is not a test of comprehensive human literary revision.

Independent EXACT physical state control confirmed the new overlay left every original physical object's real 3D position unchanged across all 197 accepted source scenes, preserved all **eleven** actual interobject Rapier contact pairs, and kept the native physics snapshot byte-identical before and after attaching/reading the sidecar. The prior hard ON grammar filter had reduced contacts from eleven to six. The non-destructive overlay therefore retains the original **three** previously independently author-annotated/contact-supported source ON category-pair matches, versus **two** after destructive filtering. This is NOT a new improvement in source-language understanding; it is evidence that avoiding premature deletion protects already valid physical information.

A numerical reporting mistake in the initial quaternion control is separately retained: dot products of identical, slightly non-unit floating-point rotations can imply about a thousandth of a radian unless normalized. The corrected version normalizes quaternion magnitudes and measures a numerical rotation deviation below one millionth of a radian; raw positions and contact sets were identical in both versions.

## Actual 3D impulse, offscreen semantic time, native return and witness privacy

The same eight *previously source-blind selected* native physically eligible source ON contacts from the prior study were replayed with four fixed predeclared genuine GameCore impulse directions, producing **thirty-two** actual physical intervention episodes on eight underlying source relations. This is NOT thirty-two independent source worlds or a general human language quality evaluation. The original source proposals, including ambiguous ones, were all retained.

Each episode started from actual native contact, recorded source syntax and physical truth separately, observed only geometrically visible information, applied the real impulse while the observer was away, settled/dematerialized actual local physical bodies, advanced the real semantic world clock seven more seconds and marked the source relationship's *current physical truth unknown* while no collider was loaded. The complete existing native Rapier/physical/observer/story record plus original undecided source interpretations was saved, restored and physically rematerialized. GameCore then determined actual contact on return and the independent physical observer was allowed to describe only what it could really see.

**All thirty-two** native/offscreen/source-hypothesis returns passed. Thirty physical interventions changed source support status to false or unknown, while two remained physically supported. All thirty-two retained the original historical prose, source hypotheses, clock and native IDs. The four source-role-approved original relationships produced **sixteen** appropriate observer relationship retractions. There were **zero** fabricated eyewitness accounts of the hidden impulse and no repeated unchanged narration. Source hypotheses remained specifically UNDECIDED through the interval of genuinely unloaded native physics, then were reevaluated after rematerialization.

The first version of this offscreen result recorded a correct physical measurement but made a separate evidence SHA-256 mistake: it hashed a JSON serialization of a Buffer rather than the actual raw file bytes. The original private raw and aggregate remain untouched; a second fully versioned replay recorded the same physical outcomes with a correctly verified raw-file SHA.

## Trusted snapshot integrity, and what it does not prove

One real original human SceneEval source was used to test a valid full native solver/source/observer/hypothesis save, then six deliberately corrupted copies: falsified stored current facts, tampered source grammar status, future world time, forged future history, altered source relation and corrupt inner native binary. All six inconsistent copies were rejected, including cases with deliberately recomputed outer JSON checksums. These are **owner-trusted internal consistency checks**; they are not authenticated security against a fully forged malicious world/save.

A prior research-only run of the new 197-scene benchmark failed every accepted source by comparing an old native object inventory that excluded provisional room boundaries against a new inventory that included them. That is an independent research *comparison bug*, not a failed game physics engine. The original raw version is retained. A separate corrected version produced all 197 actual native source/observer saves but had a null aggregate action-change field because it numerically accumulated undefined flags in scenes without a movable object; that second result is retained as a reporting error. The final exclusive third version correctly reports eight meaningful source-hypothesis changes among sixty-four actual GameCore action scenarios, with all physical native and source ID outcomes unchanged across the corrected runs. The independent read-only verifier confirms all three raw versions and checksums separately.

## Source meaning and the remaining original owner questions

The measured conclusion changes the previous engineering decision: **hard-prune source relations on an uncertain grammar role is UNSAFE**. That alternative discards original source proposals and demonstrably removed one of only three native contact-supported author ON relationships. The better default, tested here, is **non-destructive source-hypothesis persistence plus a narrow, physically guarded current narration channel**. It preserves every original source proposal, all original Rapier world geometry and current physical evidence, and abstains from pretending an unresolved original description was definitely realized.

This is not a universally proof-correct scene generator. A world whose geometry was originally created by a mistaken source parser may still contain a *physically true* relationship the author never meant. The new hypothesis ledger preserves that discrepancy; it does not automatically repair the scene. Full instance coreference, quantifier/negation scope, container geometry and physical affordances, validated 3D assets/scales/materials, unscripted offscreen actors and coherent LLM-written paragraphs are still open. The persistent world remains primarily a structured physical/semantic record with archived literary text and factual change notes, not yet the owner's intended independently sufficient expressive prose substrate.

Further work that could materially change this decision would evaluate one capable model that returns **source-linked alternative instance interpretations and explicit abstentions**, with source-independent labels on new previously unseen multi-clause paragraphs, validated interactive 3D assets and actual constraint-conditioned physical geometry. A separate language verifier must measure unintended source meaning, missing objects, observer leakage and stylistic continuity, not just valid output JSON or total created box count. Generated text is a proposal that cannot override the native GameCore world authority. Because Nitro's trading, rendering and inference workloads were active, no shared production model call or GPU experiment was performed.
