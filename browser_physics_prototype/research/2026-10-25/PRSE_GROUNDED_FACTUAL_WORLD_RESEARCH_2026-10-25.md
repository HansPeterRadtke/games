## The owner question and prior baseline

The user's original PRSE recordings on August 13 and October 9 require a persistent literary/semantic world that can materialize into ONE authoritative three-dimensional physical scene, evolve through real interactions, and return to a truthful semantic/literary state when the player leaves, saves, or returns. Renderer and voice/text presentation must not invent the physics. Optional learned skills are secondary. The new factual adapter does not replace the live game's semantic world or literary model.

## Methods: authoritative current facts, not source assertions

The new project-owned research module prse_grounded_relation_narration.mjs reads structured candidate claims from the *frozen* earlier deterministic and combined parser outputs, each preserving SceneEval source identifiers. It checks the actual currently materialized PRSELoop entities using the native GameCore/Rapier world, without introducing another physics solver.

An object claim such as “lamp on nightstand” requires unique resolved physical instances, a valid support footprint and vertical plane, and actual Rapier contact where the body is dynamic. A claim of “inside” a bookshelf is **unknown** unless the world represents a real open physical interior; a solid box does not establish inside containment. Unresolved object-instance ambiguity, missing room/door geometry, unknown camera-relative direction, and other unsupported topology become **unknown**, not narrated. Conditions and visual adjectives are not asserted just because they occur in a historical source sentence. Earlier source prose remains immutable provenance.

Only physically confirmed true relations can be rendered into present-tense factual statements. A physically visible observer receives an initial verified fact and later only meaningful deltas; after a previously seen relationship becomes false, it can say “lamp 1 no longer rests on nightstand 1”, without inventing the unseen cause, actor or time. An observer can be passed ID sets from the separately verified GameCore/Rapier ray-casting perception module. Repeated unchanged statements are suppressed, and per-observer knowledge can be serialized. This is factual English and a truth-maintenance boundary, **not** a fluent rewritten literary chapter.

For a large source test, we reused the same existing physical scene plans from the 100 human and systematically chosen 100 assisted SceneEval descriptions, for BOTH frozen parsers. Each accepted scene was stepped in native GameCore; current typed claims were classified as true, false or unknown; multiple fixed physical observer viewpoints were filtered through actual Rapier visibility; factual statements were emitted and a repeated observation tested; selected physically grounded support relations received real impulses and relation transitions were checked; then native GameCore scene and factual observer state were saved and reloaded. A separately authored geometry interpreter was compared wherever it could decide. This does not constitute independent human semantic truth or visual ground truth.

## Results: the dominant problem is uncertainty, not narrative fluency

| Physically unknown / ambiguous | 273 | 407 |

| Duplicated unchanged observer statements | **0** | **0** |

The separate geometry check agreed with 110 deterministic and 148 combined claims where the two approaches could decide, with zero disagreements. This is an internal geometry consistency test, NOT independent human interpretation of the source. Most candidates still lacked sufficient verifiable identity, topology or physical evidence. More proposed relations create more unknowns.

An independent post hoc audit used the official SceneEval annotations AFTER freezing the per-case physical claims. In the 100 human scenes the deterministic method physically verified 44 object-pair claims but only 16 matched the independent annotated object-pair labels; the combined method verified 56, of which 18 matched. In the 100 assisted scenes the respective matches were 11 out of 56 and 13 out of 77. These are optimistic unordered object-category/relation matches, not exact object-instance and direction correctness. A physically true claim absent from the published annotations is NOT automatically a hallucination; annotations can omit or ambiguously categorize relationships. Equally, physical correctness alone does not establish source-text fidelity. General literary meaning preservation is substantially unproven.

## Real save/reload discontinuity — negative and verified conservative correction

The original corpus v1 retained a concrete failed source case: external source ID 240, an actual dynamic potted plant supported by a bookshelf. Rapier confirmed contact and the factual projector reported the correct true relation before saving. Restoring the identical bodies and clock left the Rapier contact-query manifold unavailable until a subsequent real physics tick, so a brand-new query temporarily returned UNKNOWN. Merely pretending it was a new verified collision or advancing hidden physics during loading would be wrong.

The negative first output and a separate source-specific diagnostic were preserved unchanged in owner-private raw evidence. The research observer journal now uses versioned saved **previously verified physical evidence**, containing the source-relation digest, physical entity identities, three-dimensional transforms, velocities, rotations, support assignment and game time. It may carry an already verified fact across the unchanged immediate restore boundary, **without claiming a fresh collision**, only when all that evidence still matches. A new GameCore step supersedes the old proof and requires independently current physics evidence. The source-240 correction verified the original true→unknown gap without the proof, true continuity with the exact saved proof, and true native contact again after precisely one legitimate physics step. A modified source, forged physical pose and mismatched saved time were each rejected. No hidden physics step occurred.

After that correction, new versioned full-corpus v2 and v3 tests had **zero save/reload failures** in the 196 and 194 physically accepted scenes. The original v1 failure remains preserved, not recast as success. This is an internal trusted save contract, not a cryptographically authenticated adversarial file format or a proof of universal Rapier warm-start determinism.

## A genuine source-derived causal and offscreen re-entry example

SceneEval human-authored row 22 provides a recognizable lamp resting on a nightstand. The source interpreter proposed that relationship; native Rapier contact proved it in the generated scene; the near physically sighted observer received “lamp 1 rests on nightstand 1.” After a genuine GameCore impulse physically moved the lamp off the nightstand while that observer was away, no unseen cause was disclosed. The GameCore state and factual journal were saved, seven seconds of *unloaded semantic world time* elapsed, and the same entities physically rematerialized. On re-entry with both objects actually visible through physical rays, the observer received “lamp 1 no longer rests on nightstand 1.” The original source prose, object IDs and world time remained stable and no unchanged fact was repeated. The impulse was investigator-supplied, not human gameplay. This is ONE selected illustrative case, not a general source-meaning benchmark.

## Subsequent geometric falsification and final version v5

After the first corpus pass, the factual relation projector was challenged with **independently authored three-dimensional adversarial cases**. The earlier proximity predicate checked only horizontal X/Z footprints, and therefore incorrectly called two objects “near” although there was a **4.3-metre vertical surface gap**. This observed false claim remains preserved in prse_false_near_vertical_gap_prefixed_negative.json. A first correction added the vertical gap but used a max-axis horizontal gap, which still incorrectly classified two objects with **1.3-metre gaps on BOTH horizontal axes** as near; their true diagonal physical surface clearance is about **1.84 metres**, beyond the 1.5-metre near threshold. This second failure remains preserved in prse_false_diagonal_near_before_distance_fix.json.

The final projector uses **Euclidean three-dimensional axis-aligned bounding-box surface separation**, including diagonal horizontal and vertical distances, rather than maximum-axis or planar shortcuts. prse_3d_surface_distance_guard.mjs tests both old false positives plus a physically nearby positive scene in the same authoritative GameCore/Rapier world, including native save/reload, and all three pass. The before-fix negative observations are NOT counted as independent SceneEval human data. Orientation-aware shapes and rotated/compound contact geometry are still outside this axis-aligned relation check; it does not prove a universal geometry oracle.

The complete corpus was run again into new **exclusive-create v4 and final v5** raw files, with all v1/v2/v3/v4 evidence unmodified. All original externally sourced claims and save/reload outcomes stayed numerically unchanged in this particular ground-based benchmark: 196/196 and 194/194 accepted worlds preserved factual state, 100 and 133 physically true relations, 34 and 41 false, and 273 and 407 unknown. The independent annotation audit, v5 (prse_grounded_prose_source_annotation_audit_v3.json), likewise retained the human/assisted source-concordant object-pair counts 16/11 for deterministic and 18/13 for combined. This does NOT make the added falsification tests meaningless; it demonstrates that the original source sample did not expose the vertical/diagonal defect, and **a second geometric check sharing the same projection assumption cannot independently certify complete three-dimensional truth**.

The final versioned, independently verified evidence is prse_grounded_prose_evidence_manifest_v3.json and its read-only verifier, which checks exact owner-private v1–v5 raw hashes, the preserved source-240 restored contact failure, physical continuity correction, official SceneEval ZIP hash, source annotations, both proximity counterexamples and the selected physical narrative re-entry example.
