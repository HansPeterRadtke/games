Faithful objects, instance identities, geometries, room placement, physical support, contacts, forces, causal offscreen evolution, observer limitation and identity across save/reentry are the primary acceptance target. Player macros/learned habits are secondary.

**Operational result: PARTIAL, NOT DEPLOYABLE FOR ARBITRARY LITERARY WORLDS.** The existing scripted forest player game, real Nitro GameCore/Rapier, user-facing browser and bounded physical study work. A fast unsupported-input reply is not successful text-to-world execution. Keep the scripted game running unchanged, fail closed on unsupported literary source requests, and require exact source provenance, source-grounded room/instance mapping and approved collider geometry before permitting a new world to become authoritative.

## Independent primary-source authority and conflicts

External author dataset is [LEGO-Bench by Kang and colleagues, *Apples on the Table?*, EMNLP 2026](https://gyeomh.github.io/LEGO-Eval/), downloaded from [the official independent dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LEGO-Eval/LEGO_Bench). It has **130 manually authored real indoor source descriptions, 1,250 independently human-annotated source constraints, 130 constructed 3D reference worlds and 295 rooms**. The human constraint classes in the exact downloaded version are 494 Object Placement, 291 Object Selection, 272 Floor Layout, and 193 Material Selection. Official full_data.json SHA-256 is `c4cab948b923b522b9ba4991e167e1c5c7d503786f2b2e5c11a64dab89113c21`. Each original text/constraint is hashed and checked against every new server request. External human author constraints are the primary literary *intent* evidence; an example 3D asset scene is not a higher semantic authority if its contents disagree or its instance IDs are ambiguous.

The reference corpus has **6,844 raw object rows but 6,838 distinct within-scene object IDs**, with six repeated IDs. The official paper lists 2,320 receptacles and 4,518 objects, which also sums to 6,838; that arithmetic is consistent but by itself does not establish identical paper asset classification. Previous source research counted 3,894 `object_name` populated rows and 2,950 without that field, but absence of `object_name` does not mean absence of a 3D entity. **2,937** rows with an absent `object_name` have a child–parent linkage in the actual `id` field. **50** populated `object_name` values differ in type prefix from the underlying `id` type; some `object_name` values identify a compound, not the physical instance. Hence a comparison against `object_name` alone is not defensible instance-level gold.

This round scored **every reference object ID type** against exact generated kind counts in its own scene, with no scene-to-room matching, no undocumented synonyms and no fabricated geometry. The older lexical scene inventories contained **575 real native nonwall bodies**, with at most **251** strict reference-ID category-instance matches; the expanded source-only inventory contained **678 real native bodies**, at most **294** such matches, gaining 43 *possible* exact kind matches by adding 103 provisional native bodies. These are deliberately **type/quantity-only upper bounds**, not complete author-defined objects or recall: the reference includes auxiliary props and some aliases and the generated scenes omit room identity. A sensitivity analysis allowing contextual asset aliases would increase potential pairings but would not verify physical match. All 95 multi-room human source scenes still fail the single generic-room topology requirement regardless of any pairwise contact improvement.

### Real collider contact is not an authored vertical support

The frozen corrected extended source-only comparison used the same **678 body IDs, sizes and collision types in both source-only native arms**, with 38 actual GameCore/Rapier ticks each, offscreen-capable state persistence and strict no-move negatives. It had **5 actual native collider contact pairs in control and 11 in the source-conditioned alternative**, with **6 newly added pairs** and **0 formerly existing contact pairs lost**. Rechecking **real native quaternion orientation + top-face gap + full footprint + actual Rapier manifold** gives only **10 actual top supports**, not 11: the composite mouse/desk pair is a physical contact but NOT a verified vertical on-top support.

Of ten physically qualifying supports, nine have a matching human-written source claim in the **prior posthoc-selected** original author clauses, and one is the demonstrably source-unsupported lamp/bed positioning. The original author said nightstands with lamps are on either side of the bed, not that a lamp rests on the bed. The isolated physical engine may produce a real lamp–bed contact; this **cannot be elevated into author intent**. The additional six author-positive contacts were selected after observed model improvements, so **9/10 is not unbiased heldout precision**, nor can an exhaustive false-positive rate be calculated. Another one of the eleven contacts is a non-top composite and stays unproven as direct support.

Among the six new physically supported human-authored ON pairs selected in the older study, only **one** has an exact child–parent link in the release's reference `id` strings under strict type names. One has both relevant kinds present but no exact parent link; four lack a strict category for at least one endpoint. Examples: the authored projector on a *table* appears as `projector|conference_table` in the reference, a natural subtype alias; an authored computer on desk appears as a `laptop|desk` asset in another reference; a human-specified blanket on a bed lacks a matching exact `blanket` reference ID in its room. These are different ontology/asset instances, not grounds to override human instruction or certify model false positives. Correct author instance/coreference needs room-qualified IDs and asset dimensions, neither currently established for arbitrary text.

**Gold limitations:** The benchmark's 1,250 human constraints are positive and not exhaustive forbidden-parent negatives, so true population false-positive rates remain unknown. The prior eleven nonvertical/hard-negative author phrases included one physically real but non-entailed contact; their sample was selected posthoc. There is no pristine unexamined future corpus here, no independently human-labeled exact source referents for every noun phrase, no approved native asset mesh+collider metric scale or independent human correct observer-literary-change target. Do not fabricate or substitute a synthetic true-negative distribution.

## NEW actual human-user input experiment: exact deployment comparison

No replay of the earlier same-body placement experiment. Nothing was sent to the existing production PRSE server, shared Chrome session, Jetson GPU, LLM endpoint, Calendar, or real user game state. The current canonical checkout contains concurrent agent modifications; it was used read-only to launch a *private* instance, not merged/restarted or claimed to be the exact currently loaded production binary. Exact backend/game-core source SHA-256 hashes are in the private and safe aggregate evidence.

Input protocol for BOTH versions was identical in sequence and source: **130 original complete human scenes, 1,250 original independently human-annotated clauses**, total **1,380** real server text messages each, plus private save and health checks. Each message's source index, original author label type/index, UTF-16 code-unit length, input SHA-256, response type/hash and measured send-to-typed-reply time is frozen in protected per-message evidence. No source/gold was generated or rewritten; one invalid initial test could not connect due the WebSocket Origin policy and was preserved separately, followed by the versioned valid-Origin test. No failed or denied external operation was repeated.

## Decision: authority and deployment choices

The immediate correct choice is **keep real native 3D Rapier GameCore and existing scripted game**, do not turn on a false “literary importer.” The current game must either route an original description to a dedicated, provenance-checked scene-generation stage or tell the user that full literary scene import is unsupported, never pretend to act on an unrelated forest bench or plank. Any automatic generated plan is provisional and requires source-referenced room-qualified object IDs, quantities, metric/mesh collider sizes and explicit abstentions; source semantics must be independently checked against the resulting real Rapier contact/rotation/support. Preserve original author prose as historical source, authoritative physics as current world truth, and witness-only event history for observers. Rendering and freeform LLM prose can **propose but not establish** an object's real current history, authored meaning or unobserved cause.

**What would change this decision:** a truly new independently annotated *human literary source* test with exhaustive per-instance positive/negative and room topology data, accessible approved metric mesh/collider catalog, a deployed importer into the actual player's world, exact author-specific physical support under real Rapier, real observer-limited final literary prose after external impulses/hidden offscreen changes/save/reentry, matched fair-cost negative controls where permitted, and end-to-end error/latency/compute measurements. The already denied feasible-random-parent placebo is not retried here or represented as performed.

The original objective is **PARTIAL**. This round's novel contribution is precise and independently source-hashed **whole human corpus GameCore acceptance/error/latency** across two deployment code versions, correction of two spurious action dispatches, and a stricter human-source/reference-id/true-Rapier-contact authority decision. No source generation, user game, producer, GPU service, LLM or other worktree was changed.
