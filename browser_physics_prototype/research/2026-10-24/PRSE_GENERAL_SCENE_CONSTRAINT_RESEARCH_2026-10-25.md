## Methods and limits — corpus not contaminated with gold inside compiler

The previous fixed shelter translator supported four predetermined types and could not accept general SceneEval descriptions. The previous simple noun/number baseline was retained. The new **prse_semantic_constraints.py** uses a fixed human-readable noun vocabulary, conservative quantity expressions, zero/negation rules, limited modifiers, and bounded phrase-level relation extraction. It proposes entity categories, numeric lower bounds, optional properties, source spans and typed relational constraints. Gold SceneEval labels are **never passed to the interpreter**. The independent scorer, prse_semantic_constraints_eval.py, reads them afterward; the full raw 500-prompt output is stored privately rather than published.

The parser was developed with access to the first thirty-five easy human-created examples. Remaining examples are separately tabulated, but this is **not a pristine pre-registered outer holdout**, because earlier research had already evaluated the whole dataset's aggregate baseline and several initial source rows. In particular, a high first-thirty-five score cannot be reported as the system's general quality.

The interpreter's quantitative F-one and recall by independently annotated content type were:

| Source subset | Quantity F-one | Object-pair relation F-one | Room relation F-one | Attribute F-one |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First 35 hand-written development examples | 0.939 | 0.434 | 0.610 | 0.771 |
| Remaining 65 hand-written examples | 0.588 | **0.055** | 0.202 | 0.388 |
| Remaining 400 partly machine-authored examples | 0.433 | **0.058** | 0.205 | 0.152 |

The scores are exploratory and **not physical fidelity**: they measure type/count or type-pair recognition in the source descriptions. The scorer accepts directional relationships as unordered type-pairs, so even these figures can overestimate correct meaning. Several ambiguous coreferences and multi-object expressions are deliberately left unsupported. A concrete corrected input behavior is an explicit claim that there is **no coffee table**, which now produces a zero count rather than an extra physical object. Another correctly parsed quantified statement requires at least one nightstand. Neither proves the hundreds of unrecognized noun/relation types work.

## Automatic constraint-to-real-physics evaluation

The new **prse_constraint_layout.mjs** compiles extracted entities, provisional dimensions/masses and spatial constraints into physical bodies, including actual room-wall rigid bodies, using the existing GameCore / Rapier 3D world through the research-only PRSELoop. It attempts support, nearness, room-wall and relative directional constraints. It validates overlaps, floor placement, footprint, bounded IDs and object counts, rejecting or explicitly abstaining when these constraints cannot be represented. Objects remain on real XYZ coordinates and can undergo physical forces/collisions; native GameCore remains the physics authority. Defaults for dimensions, walls, orientation and masses are **approximation hypotheses**; they are not validated as an accurate architectural layout, precise visual assets or semantics understood by an LLM.

On all one hundred original human-authored descriptions, ninety-seven were physically instantiated and successfully saved/reloaded with identity and original prose stable, two rejected invalid physical placement, and one had no recognized types. On a fixed systematic sample of one hundred additional partly machine-authored descriptions (IDs 100, 104, and so on through 496), ninety-nine physically instantiated and saved, and one rejected invalid placement. These rates **only** measure that proposed bodies can be built and serialized. Their plain spatial relations and attributes were often wrong, absent or untestable.

The independent **prse_sceneeval_relation_grounding_score.py** compares the physically satisfied, automatically extracted relations against the official SceneEval annotations **after** the compiler has produced its result. It is an optimistic **direction-agnostic typed-object-pair** test, not exact relation argument matching and not visual correctness.

| Source | Gold annotated pair claims | Extracted matching pair claims | Matched AND geometrically satisfied | Grounded recall, direction ignored |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 original human descriptions | 478 | 40 | **22** | **4.60%** |
| 100 systematically sampled partly machine-assisted descriptions | 743 | 24 | **17** | **2.29%** |

This is the decisive research result. The majority of semantic constraints never reach the physics compiler. Counting ninety-seven or ninety-nine successful rigid-body arrangements as general PRSE scene understanding would be demonstrably false. Even the few credited matches ignore relation orientation, per-instance specificity and wall/camera/visual constraints. Actual fully correct meaning-preserving scene rate is unknown and must not be inferred from these upper-bound matches.

## Two selected physically demonstrated relationship updates, with annotation audit

Among the first hundred external human-authored prompts, the current parser produced only **two** geometrically valid, explicitly supported relations involving object types permitted to move: a lamp on a nightstand (source row 22) and a book on a bookshelf (source row 29). These were exhaustively tested as the tiny **eligible set**, using the same predetermined actual GameCore impulse rather than hand-drawing new after-scenes. Both real dynamic objects physically left their former supports; the resulting support-loss events updated the typed semantic relation, and saved/reloaded world facts and original entity IDs remained stable.

**Only one of the two agrees with official semantic ground truth.** Row 22's lamp-on-nightstand matches the external relation annotation. Row 29's book was placed **on** the shelf by our parser, while the official annotation says **inside** the bookshelf. The source text uses natural-language "on the bookshelf", illustrating why surface contact, enclosure and ambiguous object meaning cannot simply be guessed from a keyword. Thus the result is **two physical-causality integration checks passed, only one officially annotated relationship correctly instantiated**; it is not two successful meaning-preserving scenes.

For the lamp case, the compiler accepted actual external description text and an inferred support relation, inserted three-dimensional scene bodies, stepped GameCore, applied a physical impulse, recorded the authoritative support loss and stored a current factual description identifying the former nightstand. The original literary input remained historical source, not silently overwritten or reused as current truth. Save/re-entry preserved the support loss. This is a positive bounded causal semantic round-trip for one naturally described relation.

## Important new false semantic facts, negative preservation and corrections

A separate adversarial lift on the external nightstand/lamp scene exposed two real research-sidecar defects. First, after the lamp was physically raised **almost nine-tenths of a metre above the nightstand**, its semantic support ID still falsely claimed it was resting there because the earlier code checked only horizontal bounds. Second, the adapter could unload that moving lamp, advance five seconds of story time, and later return it with the same nonzero velocity and suspended position. The exact **pre-fix false-support and frozen-body JSON files** are retained in the owning research folder, rather than overwritten.

The isolated PRSELoop now tests both horizontal and vertical separation from an asserted support, keeps the original source relationship as provenance, and removes the current relationship when physical measurements no longer support it. Contact-verified reattachment after the object actually falls back on the same support generates a new event; save/physical replay/return preserved that correction. The recorded test had an active-physics saved/resumed lamp trajectory difference of roughly **four micrometres**, which is a single-case fidelity result, not broad bitwise determinism.

The adapter now **refuses to dematerialize an actively moving body above a conservative speed threshold** when there is no validated offscreen physical continuation for it. That prevents the previously reproduced physically impossible five-second freeze. It **does not solve** how freely moving objects should evolve while unseen: production must settle nearby physics, carry a bounded regional physical process forward, or use independently validated coarse dynamics with collision boundaries, rather than blocking indefinitely. High-speed, complex contact-rich and rotating-object support inference remain incomplete. The previous six external manually mapped scene/impact/offscreen regressions also passed with the conservative support checks.
